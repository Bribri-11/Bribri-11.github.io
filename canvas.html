My idea

Mr Sinusas wanted us to creat a peace of artwork that was meaningful and had a lesson to it. I had to think for a while about what was one thing I wanted to share with people.

After some thinking and some sketching I cam to the conclusion that I wanted people to remeber to cheris the time they were given and all the events that happen in life becase sometimes we let the moments pass us by without taking time to cherish them. To show this I created a Peace of art that I would make on Adobe and then use my building skills to make a frame for my art.

For this project specifically having a model was a necessity. The model allowed us to test our idea and take trials so we can find a trend. But sometimes constraints of a model include the materials. One constraint of testing our experiment was the fact that the model was made of cardboard so while testing and collecting data water would spill and damage the box. Because of this we would have difficulty taking conclusive data.

My group believed that a water wheel being inside of the black box would make sense because of the distribution of water coming out and coming in of the black box. Even though there are other possibilities of what could be inside of the black box, some ideas such as just a tube passing water through or a bucket would not explain why the amount of water changes the design of the black box would have to be more intricate for the water to be changing in amount. Through the process of elimination, the amount of realistic ideas of what's inside the black box would decrease. There are other ideas that could work for the black box. There is not one indefinite solution on the continents of what's inside, but our idea could be a possible solution.

21st Century Skill

I think during this assignment, I displayed problem-solving and critical thinking. I think this because throughout this assignment, we have to think out of the box a lot and use our ability to create. When I was in my group, I helped us brainstorm different ideas of what could be in the black box outside of what you would consider obvious ideas. I also helped us use the process of elimination to decide what would be the best idea for our project.

While I was creating my 3-D model I struggled a bit with understanding the website (Maya) used, but I was able to persevere through. I used my resources to ask for help when needed, but I also took accountability on my own to find tutorials on what I needed to create. Another way I showed problem-solving and critical thinking was when one of my files of my updated 3-D model was lost, I tried to find the file as best as I could, but even when I didn't, I went back and redid the work that was lost and then still continue with the work I had to do for that day.
